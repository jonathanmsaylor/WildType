# Ecological selection — running Editor evidence

Unedited 1920×1080 Game-view captures from the actual saved Creature Stage, Unity 6000.4.7f1. A temporary Editor harness initially placed the player at a meadow edge, then drove **normal motor and survival** to existing food across the boundary. Food, AI, genomes, births and deaths were not staged or replenished. The harness and its `.meta` were removed before final tests/delivery.

- [Woodland forage](01-woodland-forage.png): violet, low fernberries in the existing wooded terrain; E prompt identifies the food. First inspection run, before consumption.
- [Woodland consumption](02-woodland-meal.png): sustained virtual E through the unchanged input bridge removes fruit and restores the useful +7 energy (the existing maximum-energy clamp wastes excess nutrition).
- [Dry-region arrival](03-dry-arrival.png): real meadow-to-dry movement, gold sunpods, larger gaps, region observation and ordinary birth history.
- [Dry-region consumption](04-dry-meal.png): virtual E removes the sunpod and restores the useful +38 energy; the raw meal remains 72 nutrition, clamped by the player's reserve.
- [Recovery and natural turnover](05-recovery-turnover.png): the consumed sunpod has regrown naturally. Births 12 / deaths 1 / population 24. Generation-one #021 starved; generation-three #025 subsequently filled the vacancy. The death remains in the compact history. These AI events were not scripted.

Native automation's short E taps were missed by Editor input routing; successful eating uses a .25-second virtual key state, with input settings cloned and restored. Initial placement and scripted walking are inspection aids, not claims of a complete human-controlled map journey.
